StreamPulse Gachi customization
================================
- Channel and profile now use neutral defaults; no streamer identity is hard-coded.
- Channel now starts blank unless supplied with ?channel=TWITCH_LOGIN or saved after selection.
- Updated browser-local storage namespace to avoid restoring the old hard-coded room/profile.
- Prevents Firebase room listeners/settings writes until a valid channel is selected.
- Added a darkened, blurred custom gachi background with Billy Herrington/Van Darkholme-inspired visual collage and ♂ accents.
- Kept the existing player, hidden Twitch command bridge, Firebase queue, metadata and TikTok resolver.
